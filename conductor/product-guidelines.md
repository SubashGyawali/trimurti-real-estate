# Product Guidelines: Trimurti Real Estate

## 1. Brand Voice & Tone
Our communication should reflect the dual nature of a "Trusted Guide."
*   **The Helpful Guide (80%):** The primary voice is supportive, approachable, and jargon-free. We use "we" and "you" to create a partnership, focusing on how we simplify the complex process of finding a home.
*   **The Regional Authority (20%):** When discussing legalities, market data, and processes (especially MHADA-related), our tone shifts to be direct, precise, and authoritative to ensure client confidence.

## 2. Brand Messaging
*   **Hyper-Local Expertise:** We don't just sell "flats"; we sell homes in societies we know by name. We should reference local landmarks, neighborhood nuances, and building reputations (e.g., "Walking distance from Kandivali Station," "Well-maintained MHADA society").
*   **Emotional Connection:** Our messaging should resonate with the aspiration of Mumbai residents—focusing on security, community, and the pride of ownership in the Western Suburbs.
*   **Efficiency First:** We emphasize that our expertise "fast-paces" the process, reducing the time from viewing to moving in.

## 3. Visual & Creative Direction
*   **Realistic Context:** Avoid generic stock photos of western-style luxury apartments. Use high-quality, realistic imagery of the actual Kandivali/Malad neighborhood and genuine property interiors. This builds trust through authenticity.
*   **Professional Foundation:** Use the Slate/Professional theme (clean lines, organized data grids) to signal corporate integrity and high service standards.
*   **Localized Warmth:** Use warm accents (colors/typography) and localized icons/imagery that feel familiar and welcoming to the Indian user.

## 4. User Interaction & Conversion
*   **Frictionless Contact:** Conversion is prioritized through speed. Direct WhatsApp buttons should be ubiquitous and low-friction, acknowledging that Mumbai real estate moves fast and high-intent users prefer immediate chat.
*   **Guided Discovery:** Even within a high-speed interface, provide helpful tooltips and brief explanations to guide the user through the "Verified Listing" status and why it matters.
